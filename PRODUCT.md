# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

desktop — Electron on macOS and Windows

## Users

Keypt is for developers, designers, writers, support teams, and other keyboard-heavy desktop users who copy dozens of small things while moving between apps. The primary user is already in a flow—usually between an editor, terminal, browser, document, or chat—and needs the previous clipboard item without opening a separate workspace. Mouse users are supported, but keyboard use is the defining path.

## Product Purpose

Keypt gives the system clipboard a short, searchable memory on macOS and Windows. A global shortcut opens a compact panel, the user finds an item, and one action copies or pastes it. Success means that this loop consistently takes less than a second, the app remains quiet while idle, and users trust it enough to leave it running.

The scope is deliberately narrow: text, links, code, colors, and images; search, paste, pin, delete, and clear; a handful of privacy and retention controls. There is no dashboard, account, sync layer, AI, collaboration, or telemetry.

## Positioning

Keypt is the clipboard history that behaves like a native shortcut: local, encrypted, and gone as soon as the paste is done.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, discreet. Keypt should feel like a dependable operating-system affordance with just enough identity to be memorable. Its voice is short, plain, and honest—especially around privacy limitations and permissions.

## Anti-references

Keypt must not become a feature-dense clipboard “workspace,” a cloud-backed knowledge base, a neon developer console, a glassmorphic launcher, or a dashboard made of cards. It must not hide sensitive-data caveats behind vague “privacy-first” language. It must never decorate the core paste loop at the expense of speed or familiarity.

## Design Principles

- **Disappear into the task.** Every screen and shortcut should shorten the distance between recall and paste.
- **Keyboard is the product.** Search is focused on open, selection is predictable, and every primary action has a memorable key.
- **Privacy is behavior.** Encrypt history, respect concealed clipboard formats, exclude password managers by default, and explain what the operating system cannot reveal.
- **Honor each platform.** Use macOS menu-bar conventions and Windows tray conventions without pretending the operating systems are identical.
- **Earn every setting.** A preference exists only when it prevents a real conflict, privacy risk, or retention surprise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the renderer. Every workflow is operable by keyboard, focus is always visible, reduced-motion preferences are honored, color is never the only signal, and controls remain legible in Windows forced-colors mode. Text and control contrast must meet or exceed 4.5:1, with primary body text targeting 7:1.
